Phone: the next 20 percent

Loci feels like a phone app. The rest of Studiolo still feels like the desktop app made narrow. Six system changes close that gap, and each one fixes many screens at once.

What already works

  • The Palace opens to the whole map with one quiet card and a single bar.
  • The card stays the height you left it.
  • Typing in the bar gives Loci's suggestions, with Ask as the last row.
  • The drawer is clean: four destinations and Appearance.

Action 1

One page frame: a large title that folds into the top bar

What the live build does

  • Outside Loci, the top bar holds only ☰. The page below it starts with a desktop breadcrumb and underlined tabs.
  • On Commonplace the first entry sits 315 px below the top bar. On Learn Home it's 372 px, and on To read about 490 px.
  • On a course, the breadcrumb wraps onto two lines above the player.

Apple's ruleA large title opens the page and folds into the navigation bar as you scroll. Scope is a menu on the title, and a segmented control switches views. There are no breadcrumbs.

☰ Commonplace ▾ ⋯ ← inline title once scrolled Commonplace ▾ ← large title; ▾ = area 24 entries · 12 collections [ Everything | Collections | To read | Templates ] ← pins (All) (People) (Things) (Buildings) (More ▾) ← folds away

The change

  • Add one PhonePage component. Today, Learn, Commonplace, Course and Settings all use it under 700 px.
  • Commonplace's area becomes the title menu. Select moves into ⋯ and to a long press on an entry.
  • The segments pin under the top bar. The filter row folds on scroll and comes back on a small scroll up.

Done when

  • First content sits within 170 px of the top bar on every destination.
  • No breadcrumb renders on a phone.
  • After scrolling 60 px, the top bar shows the title everywhere.
Action 2

The bar is the only field

  • In Commonplace the bar is the search, but Return does nothing (onSubmit: () => {}).
  • On a course part the bar still says “What do you want to understand?”

Apple's ruleEach screen has one primary field, and Return always does the obvious thing.

The change

  • Remove the six duplicates on the phone. Each screen registers a bar context in their place: search collections, search To read, filter the tree.
  • Return opens the first result, or asks Loci with the query. It is never a no-op.
  • When you paste a link or type a sentence in Commonplace, the first suggestion is “Save to Commonplace”.
  • Placeholders come from the page: “Ask about Part 2…”, “Ask about Great Mosque of Kairouan…”.

Done when

  • At most one text field is visible outside the bar, open sheets aside.
  • Return changes the screen in every bar context.
Action 3

A phone-native card at every height

What the live build does

  • At full height the card shows the desktop Details panel. The title appears twice, status is a native select, and the card shows “Plan a course”, disabled, with “Course planning arrives with Learning.” Learning has already shipped.
  • The card shows zero counts, such as “0 in Commonplace · 0 Lumens”.
  • The peek shortens the name so the parent fits: “Daylight & shad… Environment & Services”.
  • In the List, a full card covers the tree.

Apple's ruleSheet content is designed for the sheet. Don't show controls that don't work. Hide empty facts. The name comes first.

The change

  • Status is a segmented control: Not started · Learning · Learned.
  • Sub-topics are rows, ending with a “＋ Sub-topic” row. Lumens are poster rows with ▶. Commonplace items appear as a picture strip. Related items are rows.
  • “Plan a course” either starts the course flow or goes away. The stale copy is deleted in all three places.
  • Zero counts never render, and the peek gives the name its full width.
  • In the List, the card stops 120 px from the top so the tree stays visible.

Done when

  • A full card has no select, no disabled button and no repeated title.
  • No “0 Lumens” or similar text appears.
  • Names up to 300 px wide never truncate at the peek.
Action 4

Rooms for Lumens, courses and items

What the live build does

  • The course page is the desktop page in one column: breadcrumb, player, title, Ask ▾, a bordered pager, chips, Feedback, then underlined tabs. That comes to about 700 px before the reading starts.
  • The Commonplace photo item is already close to a room. Back, the title and ⋯ sit in the bar, and the picture comes first. That is the model.
  • The Lumen room (Watch, Listen, Read, Live, Walk, Drive) from the prototype hasn't been built yet.

Apple's ruleContent gets the screen. Controls float over the media, you go deeper through a sheet, and playback continues in a mini player.

The change

  • Add one PhoneRoom. Back, a short title and ⋯ go in the bar. The stage is pinned under it, followed by one line of meta and one segmented control (This part | Transcript | Slides).
  • A Parts sheet replaces the pager. Swiping the stage changes part. Feedback, Report and the chips move into ⋯.
  • Leave while it's playing and the mini player docks above the bar. Tap it to resume where you left off.
  • Build order: course part first, then Lumen, then the Commonplace item and PDF.

Done when

  • The stage starts within 60 px of the safe area.
  • The reading starts within 420 px.
  • The mini player resumes within a second of where you left.
Action 5

One motion and touch system

What the live build does

  • The card uses the release speed only to pick a height. It then glides there over a fixed time, so a flick and a slow drag land the same way. Nothing rubber-bands at the ends.
TargetSizeNeeds

Apple's ruleThings you touch move on springs that keep the finger's speed and rubber-band at the edges. Every target is at least 44 pt.

The change

  • Add a 60-line spring helper. It drives the card, drawer and sheets: critically damped, taking over from the release speed, rubber-banding past the ends, and snapping instantly under Reduce Motion.
  • Hit areas grow while the shapes stay the same size. The bar goes to 44, and chips and ✕ get invisible 44 pt hit boxes.
  • Shared transitions (View Transitions API): poster → Lumen room, picture → viewer, chip → next place.

Done when

  • No interactive element's hit box is under 44 × 44.
  • A fast flick visibly overshoots and settles.
  • Reduce Motion makes changes instant.
Action 6

A phone-quality gate in CI, plus phone primitives

This is what scale means here. Design systems that hold up at scale, like Apple's UIKit and GitHub's Primer, don't rely on reviewing every screen. Their components make the right thing the default, and a check catches the rest.

The gate fails on

  • a target under 44 × 44;
  • a second text field outside the bar;
  • a breadcrumb or desktop tab strip;
  • horizontal scroll;
  • an empty top-bar title after scrolling;
  • first content more than 170 px down;
  • zero-count text;
  • serious Axe violations.

It runs every specimen at 390 × 844 in light and dark, and saves a contact sheet of every screen. The owner's iPhone check becomes a look at one image.

The primitives

  • PhonePage, PhoneRoom, PhoneSegmented, PhoneRow (44 pt, chevron, status dot) and PhoneSheet (heights plus spring). A screen built from these passes the gate by construction.

Done when

  • The gate runs in Verify. It starts as a report of today's baseline.
  • Each rule turns to failing once its action lands.

Keep exactly as it is

  • Palace and Map framing: tap to open, hold for more, the corner controls.
  • Sticky card heights and the remembered height.
  • The bar's live suggestions, with Ask as the last row.
  • The drawer and its Appearance switch.
  • Today's hierarchy: one primary button, Continue.
  • The photo viewer's layout: the model for every room.
