The phone made the foot of the screen for doing. The iPad keeps that and adds the room around it: a sidebar for going, the bar for doing, and the canvas, the room or the wall in between.

Try this

  1. Sidebar. Landscape docks it; portrait hides it behind the toolbar button or a swipe from the left edge. It holds Learn’s sections, your collections and Loci’s areas.
  2. The bar is still the one field. Press ⌘K (or Ctrl K), type, press Return. Hold ⌘ for the shortcuts; hold ⌘⇧D to talk. The mic: tap to dictate, hold to talk.
  3. Loci. In landscape the card is an inspector on the right: drag its foot to change its height, its left edge to change its width. Both stay. Turn to portrait and it is a sheet. Try List for columns.
  4. Hold a mark (right-click with a mouse) for its menu. Two fingers: pinch, twist to turn, tap to go back.
  5. Learn. Open Cooling without machines: Listen with chapters beside the hosts. Leave while it plays: it is in the sidebar’s Now Playing. Try Walk, Drive and Read.
  6. Watch. Open How a malqaf moves air, then leave: it keeps playing in a corner. Drag it. ● Live: hold the mic and interrupt.
  7. Commonplace. A wall of three or four columns. In landscape an item opens beside the wall. Drag a picture from the page on the left (Split ½) into Studiolo.
  8. Window: Slide Over and Split ½ go compact: the phone shell, same place, same card, still playing. Drag the divider across 700 px and back.
The Palace and the Map are Studiolo’s own engines. Loci’s answers, making and playback are simulated; the mic is real where the browser allows it. Open this page on an iPad to use it full screen.
