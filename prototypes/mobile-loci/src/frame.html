Studiolo on a phone

The foot of the screen is for doing, not going. One bar asks Loci, finds, makes a Lumen, searches Commonplace, dictates and creates. The four destinations move to a drawer. In Loci, what you open shows as a card that stays the height you leave it.

Try this

  1. Loci: the card starts as one line. Tap areas and topics: it stays as you left it. Drag its top up for the actions, up again for everything.
  2. Tap a chip in the card to go down without hunting for a dot. Hold a mark (right-click with a mouse) for its menu.
  3. ＋ Sub-topic: type names and press Return, or tap Loci’s suggestions. ✦ Suggest sub-topics drafts them on the Palace.
  4. Swipe from the left edge or tap ☰ for Today, Learn, Commonplace and Loci.
  5. Learn: pick Watch, Listen, Read or Live, then type in the bar and press Return. Home · Lumens · Courses.
  6. Open Cooling without machines: Listen with chapters, Walk and Drive; switch to Read. How a malqaf moves air is Watch; try ● Live.
  7. Courses: Continue opens the course room. Parts lists every part. Leave while it plays: it docks above the bar.
  8. Commonplace: the area is the title. Pick a type, its collections, Unsorted or Picks, Topic, Colour, Display. Scroll: the filters fold away.
  9. Type in the bar to search the wall. Tap a photo for the viewer (swipe, pinch, Info). Select, Shelves, Collections, To read.
  10. The mic: tap to dictate, hold to talk and let go to send.
The Palace and the Map are Studiolo’s own engines (main @ 6f8d396). Loci’s answers, Lumen playback and making are simulated; the mic is real where the browser allows it. Open this page on a phone to use it full screen.
