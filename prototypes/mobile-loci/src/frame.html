Studiolo on a phone

The foot of the screen is for doing, not going. One bar asks Loci, finds a place, dictates and creates. The four destinations move to a drawer. In Loci, what you open shows as a card, the way Maps shows a place.

Try this

  1. Swipe from the left edge or tap ☰: Today, Learn, Commonplace, Loci, and the places you opened last.
  2. In the card, tap a topic chip, Daylight & shading, to dive in without hunting for a dot.
  3. ＋ Sub-topic: type a name and press Return, again and again. Or tap Loci’s suggestions. A draft mark waits on the Palace.
  4. Hold any mark on the Palace (right-click with a mouse): open, add, make a Lumen, ask, set its status.
  5. Lumen: pick Watch, Listen or Read, and Make. Keep exploring; it lands on the topic. Play, then swipe the player down.
  6. Tap the mic and speak, or hold it to talk and let go to send. Try “suggest sub-topics” or “open passive design”.
  7. Type in the bar: places come first, then “Ask Loci”.
  8. Drag the card’s top: peek, card, full. Tap ‹ to go back, hold it for the whole trail. On a phone, a two-finger tap or a pinch out goes back too.
  9. Switch Palace · Map · List from the title. The Map shows areas only until you open one.
The Palace and the Map are Studiolo’s own engines (main @ 6f8d396). Loci’s answers are simulated; the mic is real where the browser allows it. Open this page on a phone to use it full screen.
