How Loci files what you save

Filing is fixed rules, not the model's guess, so it's the same every time and each card can say why.

What is it? Type and kind

Always a Source. The kind comes from where it was found: Open Library gives a book, YouTube a video, a link served as a PDF a PDF, anything else an article.

Which shelf? Its collection, in this order

  1. The collection you last chose for this kind in this area.
  2. Else the one you last chose for this kind anywhere.
  3. Else your starter shelf for the kind (Books, Videos, PDFs, Links), the one capture already uses.
  4. Else, if you deleted that shelf, Not on a shelf. Loci never makes a collection without your tap.

Change a card's shelf and the next save of that kind in that area follows you. There's no setting to keep.

What is it about? Topic

The place you asked from. If a closer topic exists on your map, the card says so and files there. Tagged as added by Loci.

Also on every save

  • To read: it arrives unread.
  • Where it came from: "Suggested by Loci for Courtyards", with the reason.
  • Links to things you already have, such as the Person who wrote it, or the question it may answer.

What Loci never does

  • Type a title or a link: every card comes from a catalogue and was checked to open.
  • Suggest what you already have, or anything you dismissed.
  • Save without your tap.
