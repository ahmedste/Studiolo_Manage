What the proposed player changes
  • Board slides: sketches drawn stroke by stroke as the voice names each part, with a small ink dot instead of the cursor.
  • Builds with the voice: list items, table rows and cards stay dim until they are spoken.
  • Fills the frame: sparse slides sit in the middle of the stage instead of the top.
  • Crossfade between slides, with the title arriving before the body.
  • Phrase captions: about twelve words at a time instead of the whole say.
  • Cursor fix: it jumps to its first point on a new slide instead of flying in from the corner.
  • Scrubber chapters: hover shows the slide title; , and . step between slides.
  • Paused state on the stage, and posters in Learn that show a real slide.
