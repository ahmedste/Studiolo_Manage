    How a board is written

    A board is one slide. Each line below the three hyphens is one mark: an id, a shape, its numbers on the 1280 × 720 stage, then an optional colour role, options and a label in quotes. Marks stay inside x 64–1216 and y 64–600; the band below is for captions. In the narration, [[b1.cache]] placed before a word starts drawing cache as that word is spoken. A mark with no ref is drawn straight after the one before it. A second ref to a mark sends the drawing dot back to point at it.
