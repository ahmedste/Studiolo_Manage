# Objects, boards, and questions and goals

An interactive prototype of [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-objects-questions-boards-programme.md`](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-objects-questions-boards-programme.md): the end state of the programme, where Objects, questions and goals, and a board share one Commonplace. It also draws the [collections and linking design](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-collections-and-linking-design.md) (lane C) and the [long notes and connections design](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-long-notes-and-connections-design.md) (lane N). It's drawn in `studiolo`'s current Commonplace: `app.css` is the app's own stylesheet at `main` 7c0e8732 (28 October 2026), and the markup follows the real rail, destination header, search, type row, slim bar, masonry wall, item page and Edit collection screen. The architect's sample data and photos are the app's own (`src/dev/library-sample.ts`).

It opens on **Everything**. Three sample learners, each with a board: an **architect** (*Cooling the prayer-hall courtyard*), a **lawyer** (*Duty of care after Caparo*) and a **doctor** (*Suspected PE: what to do first*). **Show phases** labels each part with the phase that builds it.

## Try this

| Where | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **The board** | Object cards carry their fields and "? · ◎" counts. The goal card is a goal inline; question cards are question inlines. The **Inside** panel lists the goal, the board's questions and the questions inside its cards |
| **Learning on a board** | On the board itself, with no separate window. Its questions are a suggested path to the goal (numbered, with a dotted route; the suggested next glows). A question card offers **▶ Answer with a Lumen**; the Lumen lands under it and **▶ Play** opens it in its card (Read, Watch or Listen), with **Did this answer it?** and **✎ Note**, which puts a note card beside it. Reorder, skip or add stops in the panel; **Recall** and **Mark the goal reached** are there too |
| **The board** | One full-screen, infinite canvas beside the rail, like Miro. The title, tools, side panel (tabs **Learning** and **Your Commonplace**) and zoom float on it, and the panel hides with one tap. **⤢ Focus** (or F) hides everything but the canvas; Esc brings it back. Drag a card's blue dot onto empty space (or press Tab) for the **add-and-link menu**: Note, Question, Lumen, or a related entry. Select several with ⇧, tidy or align them, undo with ⌘Z, find your way with the minimap; **?** lists the shortcuts. **Open** anything (double-click, Enter, a mention, Appears in) and it opens in the side panel beside its card, as a tab: notes and Objects to write in, photos to zoom, PDFs page by page and videos on a timeline, with your notes at a page or a time. **↗ On the board** turns a note into a linked card; **Full page ↗** is the only way off the board. Pan by dragging the empty canvas, zoom with Ctrl or ⌘ and the wheel, **Fit** to frame everything |
| **Placing on a board** | **Double-click empty board** and type: entries by title (accents ignored), questions, or a **New note** or **New question** from what you typed; Enter places it there. Or drag a tile from the **Your Commonplace** tab of the side panel (+ opens it) |
| **Field links** | Dashed lines are relation fields already set between two placed Objects (*Architect*, *Cites*, *Recommends*). They aren't arrows and can't be edited on the board |
| **Keep as a field** (architect) | Click the arrow *Hassan Fathy explains New Gourna Village*. It offers **Set New Gourna Village's Architect to Hassan Fathy**. Take it: a field link appears, and the Object's page shows the field |
| **Mark answered** (lawyer, doctor) | Click the arrow *Robinson explains "Is Caparo still a test?"* (or *NG158 explains "When is a D-dimer enough?"*). It offers **Mark answered in Robinson**. Take it, then double-click Robinson: its page lists the question under **Answers** |
| **Link** | Hover a card and drag its blue dot onto another card: they're linked. Click the line to add a label if you like, or ⇄ Reverse it. Both entries list the link under Appears in › On boards |
| **Ask a question** | **?** (or Q), then click the canvas. It belongs to the board and shows in Questions and goals |
| **Write a note** | **✎** (or N), then click the canvas. Start a paragraph with `@question` or `@goal`: the note is saved to Commonplace with them inside |
| **Place** | **+ Place**: Objects by collection, notes, Sources, and **Questions** written elsewhere. A placed question still belongs to its note |
| **Status** | Click any **Open ▾** or **Active ▾**: mark answered, park, answer in a line, or **Answered in** an Object or a board. It changes everywhere it shows |
| **An Object's page** | **What I took from it** is the same block editor as a long note: click and type, `@` for a question or an entry. The rail is what it is (fields, links, **Inside**); the foot is **Notes about it** (margin notes, **Write about it**) and **Appears in** (*Mentioned in* with the sentence, *Answers*, *On boards*) |
| **A long note** | **Long note** in the bar. A Craft-like page, always editable: `/` inserts a block (heading, list, quote, divider, image, entry card, question, goal); `@` makes a question or goal, or links an entry by name; select words for **B**, *I*, **Link to entry**, **Ask about this**, **Margin note**; hover a block for **+** and **⋮⋮** (drag, Turn into…, Duplicate, Delete). **On this page** on the left; **Details** opens the rail; **⤢ Focus** (Ctrl or ⌘ Shift F) leaves only the writing. Click a mention for its card |
| **Collections** | The header tab: the real Collections page with kind chips, **Edit** on each tile, and **+ New collection** (three steps: what it holds, a starter for your field, name and colour) |
| **The collection studio** | **Customize** in the bar, **Edit** on a tile, or click a property's name on any Object page. Four tabs, each a picture of your own entries: **Card** (layout, the fields under the title), **Page** (the top, sections in order, *Write about it* headings), **Fields** (drag to reorder, click to edit, suggestions to add; hide before delete), **Settings** (name, colour, cover, sensitivity, merge). Changes save as you go; **Undo** takes back the last |
| **Templates** | The header tab (or **Templates** in the bar): one gallery of things to start from, each with a picture of what it makes and **Use**. **Collections**: three generic templates for any field (**People, Precedents, Cases**), then the pre-made ones for *Architecture · Law · Medicine*; **Use** opens New collection filled in; **Long notes** (general ones and your collections' *Write about it* headings) starts a note; **Boards** (*Toward a goal, Compare two, Cause and effect*) makes a board with its cards and arrows. **? How Commonplace works** is the short explainer, also behind the **?** in the wall's bar. The rail's *Template* row is now **Kind** |
| **Appears in** | **↩ Appears in 3 places** at the top of any entry, and the section at its foot: *Mentioned in* (with the sentence), *Shown as a card in*, *Shown in* (photos), *Quoted in* (sources), *Answers*, *On boards*. Each row opens the place at the exact spot, highlighted. Try Hassan Fathy, the arcade photo, or the lecture-notes PDF |
| **Write about it** | On any Object, **Write about it** starts a long note about it, with its card and the collection's headings (set in **Customize › Page**). It's listed under **Notes about it** |
| **A Source** | Open the lecture or the judgment: a question in a margin note at 12:40 or p. 21. **? Question** adds one at the current moment (phase Q3) |
| **Questions and goals** | **Questions** in the bar. Four tiles (open, goals, answered, parked); goals as cards with a progress ring, their questions inside and a **Next** line; the other questions as a wall of cards by topic. **Answered** reads as *What you've found out*. Every card opens where it was written, highlighted. **List** keeps the old list |
| **Everything** | The real wall: cards count what's open inside them (? and ◎); **Boards** is a type; **Has ▾** filters to open questions or goals |
| **Collection views** | **Objects › Buildings** (or Cases, Guidelines): **Cards, Table, Columns, Timeline**. Drag a card between columns to change its Choice field |
| **Customize** | Edit collection with the new field types (Choice, Relation, Long text…), up to 24 fields, the page's top, and Sensitivity |

All content is example data; the legal and clinical content is for illustration only and isn't legal or clinical advice. Nothing calls a model; Loci's actions (Explain this, Find resources, Plan a course) say which phase builds them. Changes last until you reload.
